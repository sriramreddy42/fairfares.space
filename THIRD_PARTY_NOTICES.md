# Third-party data notices

## GeoNames

FairFares can use the GeoNames geographical database for its offline city and
neighborhood catalogue. GeoNames data is licensed under the Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 License. See [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/) and the
[CC BY 4.0 licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The application does not call the GeoNames web service at runtime. Database
dumps are imported by an explicit maintenance command.
