FairFares account controls

Delete your FairFares account.

You can initiate deletion here or from Account → Delete account in the FairFares mobile app.

$message

What happens

  • Your request receives a tracking number and a completion deadline no later than 30 days after submission.
  • FairFares deletes or de-identifies your profile and associated personal data that is not required for an ongoing service or legal obligation.
  • Booking, payment, tax, rental agreement, insurance, safety, fraud-prevention, chargeback, and dispute records may be retained only for their required retention period.
  • Content already shared with another user may remain in de-identified form where deletion would affect that user’s lawful records or conversation integrity.
  • You will receive a confirmation at the email address associated with your account.
