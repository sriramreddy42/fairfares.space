First opens → accounts → housing → Chitthi → carpool → rentals → return

Stage reach counts unique people independently; it is not a forced sequence. This shows where people actually engage after installing. “Activated” means a person posted housing, sent a message, joined a Chitthi community, or started/completed a rental booking. A confirmed housing connection is counted only when the listing owner explicitly marks their own request as found or their own place as filled. D7 return counts signed-in people whose first app open was 7–90 days ago and who opened the app again on day 7–14. Search text, locations, listing details, and message contents are never collected. First installs are measured by the first successful app open; historical activity is not backfilled.
