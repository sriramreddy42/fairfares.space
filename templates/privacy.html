FairFares Privacy Policy

Your data should never be a mystery.

This policy explains what FairFares LLC collects through the FairFares website and mobile app, why we use it, when we share it, and the choices available to you.

Effective and last updated: July 23, 2026

1. Who we are and the scope of this policy

FairFares LLC (“FairFares,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) provides vehicle search, rental booking and management, payments, identity and pickup workflows, accommodations, rides, community features, and trip-planning tools. This policy applies to the FairFares website, Android and iOS apps, and related customer-support interactions. It does not control a third party’s own service or privacy practices.

2. Data we collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect:

  • Account and contact data: name, email address, phone number, address, date of birth, password in hashed form, profile photo, communication preferences, and student email or ID information.
  • Booking and transaction data: vehicle selections, pickup and return locations and times, booking history, discounts, charges, payment status, receipts, refunds, rental agreements, signatures, support records, and insurance information. Payment-card details are entered into and processed by Stripe; FairFares generally receives transaction identifiers and status rather than full card numbers.
  • Identity and safety data: driver’s-license details and images, selfies or identity-verification results, insurance documents, vehicle inspection images, damage or accident information, odometer and fuel records, and fraud or eligibility review results. Some of this may be government-issued identification or other sensitive data.
  • Location and trip data: locations you enter, trip routes and stops, ride origins and destinations, accommodation areas, check-ins, and approximate or precise device location when you choose to enable a feature that requests it. If you explicitly start Chitthi live-location sharing, encrypted location updates are shared with the conversation you selected until you stop sharing, the chosen duration expires, or the app can no longer provide updates. You can deny or revoke device location permission in your device settings.
  • Community and uploaded content: profile information, listings, posts, messages, comments, reactions, reports, photos, videos, and other files you submit. Content shared in a community or listing may be visible to other users as indicated by that feature.
  • Device, usage, and diagnostics data: IP address, browser or app type, device and operating-system information, pages or screens viewed, interactions, referring pages, timestamps, cookie or session identifiers, crash information, and similar logs. We use Google Analytics on non-admin web pages.
  • Communications: messages and attachments sent to support, feedback, survey responses, and email or text delivery and preference records.

We collect data directly from you, automatically from your browser or device, from staff completing an authorized rental workflow, and from service providers such as payment and identity-verification providers.

3. How we use data

  • create and secure accounts; authenticate sessions; and provide requested app features;
  • search availability, create and manage bookings, coordinate pickup and return, process payments and refunds, and provide receipts and agreements;
  • verify identity, driver eligibility, licenses and insurance; prevent fraud; protect users, vehicles, and the service; and investigate incidents;
  • provide maps, trip planning, check-ins, rides, accommodations, communities, support, and personalized service content;
  • send transactional messages and, with your choice where required, promotional email or text messages;
  • operate, troubleshoot, analyze, and improve FairFares, including measuring feature and marketing performance;
  • comply with law, enforce agreements, resolve disputes, and establish or defend legal claims.

When FairFares offers AI-assisted support, recommendations, or document prefill, the text, images, or documents submitted to that feature may be processed by an AI service provider. AI-generated document suggestions are reviewed in the applicable workflow and are not a DMV verification.

4. When we share data

We do not sell personal data. We may disclose only the data reasonably needed to:

  • Service providers: hosting and storage providers; Stripe for payments and identity verification; Google services for file storage, maps, places, and analytics; email and communications providers such as Resend or configured mail providers; customer-support and internal-collaboration tools; AI or OCR providers; and approved identity or license-verification providers.
  • Rental operations: vehicle providers, insurers, roadside or claims partners, and authorized staff when needed to fulfill or support a booking.
  • Other users: information you intentionally publish or share through listings, rides, chats, communities, or other collaborative features.
  • Legal and safety purposes: law enforcement, courts, regulators, insurers, or other parties when we reasonably believe disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety, property, users, or the public.
  • Business changes: an actual or proposed buyer, successor, investor, or adviser in a merger, financing, reorganization, or sale, subject to appropriate confidentiality protections.

These recipients may process data in other U.S. states or countries under their own privacy terms and our applicable agreements.

5. Retention and deletion

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above. Retention varies by record: active account and operational data may remain while you use FairFares; session and verification tokens may expire sooner; and booking, payment, identity, agreement, insurance, safety, tax, fraud-prevention, and dispute records may be retained longer when required for legal, accounting, insurance, security, or claims purposes. We delete or de-identify data when it is no longer reasonably needed, subject to backups and legal obligations.

You may request account and associated-data deletion through the mobile app or the dedicated account deletion page. We verify requests through the authenticated account, provide a tracking number and deadline, delete data that we are not required or permitted to retain, and explain material data we must keep. Deleting an account may cancel access to active services and cannot erase content already lawfully retained by another user.

6. Your choices and rights

You can update certain profile and booking information in FairFares, manage marketing preferences through available controls or an email unsubscribe link, and control cookies or device permissions through browser and device settings. Disabling a permission may prevent the related feature from working.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, portability, or restriction of certain processing, and to opt out of targeted advertising, sale, or certain profiling where applicable. FairFares does not sell personal data. Submit a request through Contact, Account deletion, or hello@fairfare.space. We may need to verify your identity and may deny or limit a request where the law allows.

If we deny a privacy-rights request, reply to the decision with the word “Appeal” and explain why you believe it should be reconsidered. We will review the appeal and provide a written response within the period required by applicable law. You may also contact your state attorney general or other applicable regulator.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal data, including HTTPS in transit, access controls, hashed account passwords, and use of specialized payment and identity providers. No storage or transmission method is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Protect your credentials and contact us promptly if you believe your account has been compromised.

8. Children’s privacy

FairFares is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. Rental eligibility may require a higher minimum age under applicable provider rules and law. If you believe a child under 13 provided personal data, contact us so we can investigate and delete it as appropriate.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as FairFares, our data practices, or legal requirements change. We will post the revised policy at this URL, update the “last updated” date, and provide additional notice when required.
