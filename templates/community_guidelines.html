FairFares Community Guidelines

Be real. Be respectful. Keep people safe.

These rules apply to profiles, listings, rides, Chitthi messages, groups, reviews, polls, files, and other community activity.

Effective and last updated: August 10, 2026

1. Use your authentic identity

Do not impersonate people or organizations, misrepresent a listing or vehicle, fabricate availability, ratings, credentials, or affiliations, or use contact information that is not yours.

2. Respect people

No harassment, threats, stalking, bullying, hate speech, discriminatory exclusion, sexual exploitation, unwanted sexual content, or disclosure of another person’s private information. Housing and rides must comply with applicable anti-discrimination laws.

3. Protect safety

Do not promote violence, dangerous driving, impaired driving, weapons misuse, human trafficking, illegal drugs, self-harm encouragement, or instructions that create a credible risk of harm. Do not pressure users to share live location, identity documents, access codes, or financial information.

4. Keep listings and offers honest

Describe prices, fees, dates, locations, rules, vehicles, rooms, and availability accurately. No bait-and-switch offers, fake deposits, advance-fee scams, pyramid schemes, counterfeit goods, or payment redirection intended to defeat safety controls.

5. No spam or platform abuse

Do not mass-message users, scrape data, automate abusive activity, manipulate polls or ratings, evade blocks, repeatedly repost removed content, distribute malware, or interfere with FairFares systems.

6. Report and block

Use Report for suspicious, unsafe, illegal, or rule-breaking content. Use Block to stop unwanted direct contact. Reports are sent for review; blocking does not replace contacting emergency services or law enforcement when necessary. Knowingly false or retaliatory reports are prohibited.

7. Enforcement

FairFares may warn users, limit reach or features, remove content, preserve evidence, suspend or terminate accounts, and contact appropriate authorities when reasonably necessary. Decisions consider severity, context, history, risk, and legal obligations.

8. Questions and appeals

For safety concerns, moderation questions, or an appeal, use Report an issue in the app or email hello@fairfare.space. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
