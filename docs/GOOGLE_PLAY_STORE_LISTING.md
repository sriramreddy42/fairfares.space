# FairFares — Google Play Store listing

## App name

FairFares

## Short description

Find housing, roommates, carpool rides, rental cars, and community support.

## Full description

FairFares brings housing, roommates, carpool rides, rental cars, and private community messaging together in one place.

HOUSING & ROOMMATES
Browse housing and roommate listings by location, price, and preferences. Looking for a place, a roommate, or someone to take over a room? Connect directly with people in your community.

CARPOOL
Find people travelling along your route or offer a ride when you are already making a trip. Coordinate pickup, drop-off, and trip details in Chitthi.

RENTAL CARS
Browse available rental cars for short and longer trips. Review prices and pickup details before booking.

CHITTHI
Message FairFares members one-to-one or in groups to coordinate housing, rides, and listings.

Whether you are relocating, searching for housing, looking for roommates, planning a trip, or sharing a ride, FairFares brings these services together in one place.
